How to get in
The mod

A whole kingdom,
rebuilt from scratch

BCR started on 1.16.5. For 1.21.1 it's been rewritten from the ground up, with new spells, new visual effects, new systems and a story to play through.

01

30 grimoires · 162 spells

From Fire and Lightning to Time, Spatial and Anti-Magic. Every magic type has its own spell set, and every spell's effects were redone from scratch.

02

The grimoire ceremony

Your journey starts in the tower. A cinematic cutscene rolls your magic type, and that grimoire is yours. Some magic is rarer than others.

03

Storyline & one central GUI

A built-in storyline with NPCs and cutscenes, plus one menu for your stats, spell unlocks, quests, events and dungeons.

04

The Clover Kingdom

A 12,000 × 12,000 map with the Clover Castle, all nine guild bases and quest locations everywhere. Each guild base is packed with NPCs.

05

Dungeons & bosses

Clear dungeons and fight bosses in the arena, with fully reworked entities and highly detailed boss-fight effects.

06

Transformations

Devil forms, Spirit Dive and the Water Valkyrie, each with its own cinematic transformation.
