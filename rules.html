Code of the Magic Knights

Rules & FAQ

Keep the kingdom fair and fun. By joining the server or the Discord, you agree to follow these rules.

Server rules Discord rules Beta rules Punishments FAQ

Server rules

These apply everywhere in-game: the overworld, guild bases, dungeons and events.

Respect everyone

No harassment, hate speech, discrimination or threats. Banter is fine, bullying isn't.

No cheating

No hacked clients, x-ray, auto-clickers, macros or mods that give you an unfair advantage.

Report bugs, don't abuse them

Found a dupe, exploit or broken spell? Report it to staff. Abusing it will get you banned.

Fight fair

No combat logging, spawn-camping, or repeatedly hunting down new players.

No griefing or stealing

Don't destroy, steal from or vandalize other players' builds, bases or items.

Keep chat clean

No spam, excessive caps, NSFW content or advertising other servers.

Appropriate names, skins & builds

Everything you show in-game has to be appropriate for all ages.

No real-money trading

Don't sell accounts, items or ranks for real money outside the official store.

Staff have the final say

Follow staff instructions in-game. If you disagree with a decision, appeal politely in the Discord.

Discord rules

The Discord is the heart of BCR. Help keep it a good place to hang out.

Follow Discord's Terms

Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines apply at all times.

Spoilers go in #spoilers

New episodes and chapters are only discussed in #spoilers. Posting them in #general gets you muted.

Use the right channels

Keep conversations on topic, and post suggestions and bug reports in their own channels.

No self-promo or DM ads

Don't advertise servers, socials or anything else, in channels or in DMs.

Don't spam pings

Only ping staff or the developer when it's actually needed.

Beta rules

Extra rules for closed beta testers.

Never share or post the mod

The beta build stays with beta testers. Posting it online gets it taken down and your account banned on the site you posted it to.

Report bugs properly

Say what happened, where, and how to reproduce it. Screenshots and clips help a lot.

Expect a wipe

All beta server data is wiped before release. Testers keep an exclusive cosmetic armor piece.

Punishments

Most issues follow the ladder below. Serious offenses like cheating, hate speech or leaking the beta mod can skip straight to a ban.

FAQ

Common questions about the mod and server.

When does BCR release?
The closed beta runs Oct 16 – 23 (it could shift one week to Oct 23 – 30). Planned public release: End of October 2026. Follow the Discord for exact dates.
How do I get into the beta?
Join the Patreon ($5/month) for guaranteed access, or win a free spot in one of the Discord giveaways. More info on the Join page.
What Minecraft version do I need?
Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1. The mod loader will be announced before release. The install guide will list it once it's final.
Do I need any other mods?
The goal is zero dependencies: install the BCR mod and play. If anything else turns out to be required, it'll be listed in the install guide.
Can I play in singleplayer?
Yes. The guild bases and the Grimoire Tower are planned to generate as structures in normal worlds, so you can explore them in singleplayer too.
How many magic types are there?
30 grimoires with 162 spells in total. Browse them all on the Magic page. More magic types can be added after release.
Will my beta progress carry over?
No. The beta server is wiped before release, but every tester keeps an exclusive cosmetic armor piece.
Wasn't BCR a 1.16.5 mod?
The original version was. The new BCR is a full remake for 1.21.1, with a new GUI, a quest system, cutscenes, reworked magic tools and new effects for every spell.
Can I join the staff or media team?
Staff applications open in rounds, and they're announced in the Discord. The media team is a paid role that helps make short-form videos. Message the owner on Discord with examples of your work. See the Team page.
Where can I watch gameplay?
Devlogs are on YouTube, and spell showcases, transformations and the trailer are on TikTok and Instagram.
